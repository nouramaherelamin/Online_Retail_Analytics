# Project Summary

## Objective
Analyze transactional retail data to understand revenue, orders, products, customers, geography and demand timing.

## Deliverables
- Clean analytical dataset
- KPI tables
- Product analytics
- Country analytics
- RFM customer analytics
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard
- Complete Jupyter Notebook

## Core KPI Definitions
**Revenue:** Quantity × UnitPrice for valid positive sales transactions.

**Orders:** unique InvoiceNo among valid sales.

**AOV:** Revenue / Orders.

**Customer:** unique CustomerID among transactions with available CustomerID.
